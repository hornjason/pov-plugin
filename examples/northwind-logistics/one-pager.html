Three projects, one platform decision

A perspective for Northwind Logistics

Your Environment

The regional merger left two incompatible environments. Project Lantern consolidates them this spring, the driver app rewrite has waited on parity since the deal closed, and the board wants a dispatch routing model live by Q3.

What We're Seeing

Three rooms, three budgets, and a plan that assumes independence. The rewrite is blocked by the same split environment Lantern exists to fix, and the routing model needs capacity nobody allocated.

Consolidation is being treated as a cost project. It is the capacity event that makes the other two possible. Finish Lantern and the rewrite unblocks itself, because staging finally matches production, and the compute the merger was wasting becomes the compute the routing model runs on.

A Path Forward

  1. Finish Lantern on one platformConsolidate both estates onto the container platform rather than reconciling two virtualization stacks.
