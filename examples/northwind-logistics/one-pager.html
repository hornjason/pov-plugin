Three projects, one platform decision

A perspective for Northwind Logistics

Your Environment

The regional merger left two incompatible environments running side by side. Project Lantern consolidates them this spring. The driver app rewrite has been waiting on environment parity since the deal closed. And the board has asked for a dispatch routing model in production by Q3.

What We're Seeing

These three are planned in three different rooms, on three different budgets. The sequencing assumes they are independent. They are not: the app rewrite is blocked by the same split environment Lantern exists to fix, and the routing model needs capacity that nobody has allocated yet.

Consolidation is being treated as a cost project. It is the capacity event that makes the other two possible. Finish Lantern and the app rewrite unblocks itself, because staging finally matches production — and the compute the merger was wasting becomes the compute the routing model runs on.
